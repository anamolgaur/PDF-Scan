Everything a scanner should do

Finds the page for you

An on-device AI model spots the page's corners, even on a cluttered or white desk, then snaps the crop onto the real edges. Auto capture takes the shot when you hold still.

Reads every page

Text recognition in English plus 100+ free downloadable languages, from Hindi, Tamil and Bengali to Chinese, Japanese and European languages. Every PDF is searchable.

Mark up and sign

Pen, highlighter, boxes and arrows. Draw your signature once and place it anywhere. Edit existing PDFs too, and their original text stays sharp.

Clean, sharp PDFs

Filters for colour, greyscale and black-and-white scans, glare reduction, an eraser for smudges, three file sizes and AES-256 passwords.

ID cards, QR, books

Front and back of a card on one A4 page at real size. QR and barcode reading with Wi-Fi and UPI support. Split book spreads and flatten curved lines.

Locked and private

Lock the app or single folders with fingerprint, face or PIN. Hide it from recent apps. A recycle bin keeps deleted pages for 30 days.

Crops that land on the edge

PDF Scan uses DocAligner's most accurate corner model, run on your phone's processor. It sees the photo at its real shape, and the classic detector stays on as a backup. Then each side is snapped onto the page's real edge, ignoring text lines and shadows.

Questions

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

PDF Scan is a personal, open-source project shared with family, friends and anyone who wants it. Installing the APK directly from this page or GitHub is safe; Android just asks you to allow it once.

Does it really work without internet?

Yes. Scanning, AI page finding, text recognition, editing and search all run on your phone. Internet access is off by default and is only used, after asking, to download extra recognition languages.

Why is the download so big?

Most of it is the AI model that finds pages and the text-recognition engine. They are big so they can run fully on your phone instead of on someone's server.

Can I edit PDFs I already have?

Yes. Open any PDF and mark it up, sign it, turn, reorder or remove pages. Pages you only mark up keep their original sharp, selectable text.

Is it really free?

Yes, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. The code is open source under the Apache License 2.0.
